Full 3-D Numerical Digital Twin

A transient 3-D finite-volume model of a cold room. It covers airflow, pressure, heat, moisture, condensation and frost, plus injection, leakage, doors, walls, internal loads, products and unit coolers. The model computes the full psychrometric state of every cell at every saved time.

0%

A. Simulation setup

Moisture can be given as RH, humidity ratio, dew point, vapour pressure or specific humidity. It is converted to the transported humidity ratio ω. Zones and vertical gradients can be added in the Scenario JSON tab.

Walls and ceiling use a single insulated-panel layer. The floor is a slab on ground (edit per-surface layers in the JSON tab). Heat flux is q = U (Tsol-air − Tair), with 1/U = 1/hi + ΣL/k + 1/ho. Envelope leakage is the effective leakage area per m² of surface.

In auto mode Δt adapts to the CFL + diffusion limit of the explicit scheme. In fixed mode the run stops with a diagnostic if Δt becomes unstable.

B. Air sources, openings & loads

Faces: W (x=0), E (x=Lx), S (y=0), N (y=Ly), B (floor), T (ceiling). Choose "interior" for a point/jet source. Positions are in metres. Specify one flow, one moisture and one thermal quantity; the rest is derived.
