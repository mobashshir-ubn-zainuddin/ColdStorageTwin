The Cold Storage Digital Twin

What is a digital twin?

A digital twin is a virtual representation of a physical system that reproduces its structure and behaviour closely enough to predict how it will respond to changes. For a cold store, the twin is a physics-based numerical model. It solves the conservation laws for air mass, momentum, energy and water vapour inside the chamber. From them it reconstructs temperature, humidity, pressure and airflow at every point, not only where sensors happen to be installed.

The model answers one central question: given the geometry, initial state, wall construction, air injection, leakage, door openings, heat loads and refrigeration, how do temperature, pressure, airflow, moisture, humidity, energy and condensation evolve at every location over time?

How the project has evolved
Stage 1: diffusion prototype (Dashboard)

An explicit finite-difference solver for coupled heat and moisture diffusion in still air. It uses fixed (Dirichlet) wall temperatures, a Magnus-formula dew point, and a Von Neumann stability check (r ≤ 1/6). It is still available as the reference baseline.

Stage 2: full coupled model (Full 3-D Twin)

A transient 3-D finite-volume model. It adds airflow, a pressure field, buoyancy, air injection, leakage and door openings, insulated walls, internal heat sources, stored products and thermostat-controlled unit coolers. Every psychrometric quantity is available at every cell and saved time.

Governing equations

Continuity (low-Mach, room dry-air density ρ̄(t))

∇·u = qsrc − (1/ρ̄) dρ̄/dt

Momentum (Boussinesq)

∂u/∂t + (u·∇)u = −∇p/ρ̄ + ∇·[(ν + νt)∇u] − g (ρ − ρref)/ρref ẑ + Su

Energy

∂(ρ̄ cp T)/∂t + ∇·(ρ̄ cp u T) = ∇·(keff∇T) + SQ + L (Scond − Sevap)

Moisture

∂(ρ̄ ω)/∂t + ∇·(ρ̄ u ω) = ∇·(ρ̄ Deff∇ω) + Sω + Sevap − Scond

Room pressure (implicit dry-air balance)

[Mda(P) − Mdan] / Δt = Σopenings ṁda,i(P),   Mda(P) = P V / [Rda TK (1 + 1.6078 ω)]

Opening flow ṁ = sign(ΔP) Cd A √(2ρ|ΔP|), with ΔP(z) = Pout − Pin including hydrostatic columns.

Verification
Analytical solution

A cosine temperature/humidity profile in a sealed box must decay as e−Γπ²t/L². Refining the mesh from 8 to 32 cells shows an observed order of accuracy of p ≈ 2.0–2.2, as expected for this second-order scheme.

Conservation

With every mechanism active (injection, doors, leaks, people, products, coolers), the dry-air, water and energy inventories match their cumulative inflows/outflows to ≈ 10⁻¹² %. The continuity residual stays at round-off.

Physical checks

Psychrometrics match ASHRAE values. A still isothermal room stays at rest. A sealed room pressurises by the ideal-gas law under injection. Doors draw warm air in at the top and spill cold air at the bottom.

What you can do in the Full 3-D Twin
  • Set up any rectangular room, mesh, initial state, wall construction and outside climate.
  • Add as many injections, leakages, door events, heat sources, product blocks and unit coolers as needed, each with its own location, conditions and timing.
  • Watch any of 30 fields in 3-D or on horizontal/vertical slices, with a time slider, animation and velocity vectors. These include temperature, RH, dew point, wet bulb, enthalpy, pressure, velocity, energy and condensation.
  • Probe any point for its complete psychrometric state, its time history and its trajectory on a psychrometric chart.
  • Assess cooling time, heat loads (walls, infiltration, internal, coil), condensate and frost, infiltrated air, room pressure and conservation errors; export the time series as CSV.
Why it matters for cold storage
  • Hidden warm spots: resolve temperature non-uniformity that a sparse sensor array misses.
  • Door and infiltration losses: quantify the heat and moisture load of every opening, and the value of strip curtains.
  • Frost and condensation risk: locate where surfaces fall below the dew point and how much frost accumulates on walls and coils.
  • Refrigeration sizing: test unit-cooler capacity, placement and set-points against realistic transient loads.
  • Product protection: follow how warm incoming pallets cool and how they affect the surrounding air.
